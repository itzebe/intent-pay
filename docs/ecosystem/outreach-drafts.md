## 1. Monad ecosystem introduction

> Hello Monad team,
>
> I have built **Intent Pay**, an intent-based payment layer on Monad Mainnet
> (chain id 143). A user states what the recipient should receive — recipient,
> amount, token — and the app resolves the asset to spend, the Uniswap V3 route,
> and the transaction, which the user approves in their own wallet. Settlement is
> verified from the confirmed receipt.
>
> It is live at https://intent-pay-roan.vercel.app and fully non-custodial. I would
> welcome a conversation about how a payment-focused application fits the
> ecosystem, and about any programme intended for builders at this stage.
>
> Thank you.

## 2. Developer community introduction

> Hi all,
>
> I maintain **Intent Pay**, an open payment layer on Monad. For developers there
> is a versioned HTTP API (`/api/v1`) and a TypeScript SDK that take a payment
> intent from creation to independently verified settlement. `GET
> /api/v1/capabilities` reports exactly what is implemented.
>
> It is mainnet-only and non-custodial; the API key can prepare a transaction but
> can never sign or send one. If you are building anything that needs to accept or
> make a payment on Monad, I would value your feedback on the API shape.
>
> Docs: https://intent-pay-roan.vercel.app/developers

## 3. Potential merchant introduction

> Hello,
>
> I built **Intent Pay** so a business can accept crypto without asking the
> customer to hold the exact asset. You create an order, share a payment link or
> QR, and receive the token you asked for; the customer pays with their own
> wallet and settlement is verified on-chain.
>
> There is no cost to try it and no custodial risk — you keep your own wallet. If
> you would like a walkthrough of the merchant console, I am happy to arrange one.
>
> Console: https://intent-pay-roan.vercel.app/merchants
